Research

  • Optimization of Fragmented Spectrum in Elastic Optical Networks Using Pattern Recognition

    MSc Thesis, University of Qom, 2022 — Supervisor: Dr Mohsen Nickray

    Proposed a pattern-recognition approach to reduce spectrum fragmentation and connection blocking in elastic optical networks (EONs). Real-world IPTV and VoD traffic datasets were analyzed to identify peak-load periods and recurring packet patterns between node pairs; a decision-tree model then predicted the transmission threshold and average frequency-slot demand for upcoming time intervals, so that spectrum could be reserved in advance. The method combines Push-Pull defragmentation with a greedy route-grouping algorithm and size-based connection priority, and was evaluated through OPNET simulation on the 14-node NSFNET topology.
    Keywords: Elastic Optical Networks, Spectrum Defragmentation, Pattern Recognition, Decision Trees, OPNET
